ELDEN RING NEWS NETWORK Privacy Policy Last Updated: November 6, 2026 1. Introduction ELDEN RING NEWS NETWORK is an independent Telegram news bot providing gaming news, updates, and information related to Elden Ring and FromSoftware. We respect user privacy and aim to collect only the information necessary to operate our services. 2. Information We Collect Our bot may store the following information: - Telegram User ID - User language preference - Telegram Group ID - Group language preference - Previously delivered article links We do not intentionally collect phone numbers, passwords, payment information, or private account credentials. 3. How We Use Information Collected information is used exclusively to: - Remember user language preferences. - Configure automatic news delivery in groups. - Prevent duplicate news articles. - Maintain the bot's functionality. 4. Data Storage Information is stored in a local SQLite database on the server hosting the bot. We do not sell or rent user information to third parties. 5. Third-Party Services Our bot retrieves news through RSS feeds and may link to external news publishers. When users open external links, those websites may apply their own privacy policies and data collection practices. 6. Data Retention Stored information is retained as long as necessary to operate the bot. Users can stop interacting with the bot at any time. Group administrators can remove the bot from their groups. 7. Security We take reasonable measures to protect stored information and restrict access to bot credentials and database files. 8. Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated when bot features or data practices change. 9. Contact For privacy-related questions, please contact the bot owner through the bot's official contact channel. --- ELDEN RING NEWS NETWORK Independent Gaming News Project.
